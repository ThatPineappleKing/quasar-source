      Air Traffic Control

      Send links to a site straight to the Space you choose. This applies to links from other apps, links that open a new tab, and addresses typed in the command bar. Each Space has its own logins.

        Extensions

        Chrome extensions run in every Space (not private windows). Their buttons appear next to the address bar.

        Add from the Chrome Web Store

        Open the extension's page on chromewebstore.google.com, copy its address, and paste it here (or paste its ID).

        · ·

            Electron supports most of what extensions need, but not everything Chrome does: some extensions (or parts of them) may not work. Quasar warns you before installing one that needs something it doesn’t have. Untick a Space under an extension to keep it out of that Space (Spaces that share a profile share their extensions).

            Updates

            The engine (Electron, which brings Chromium's security fixes) updates itself: see Settings › Updates in the sidebar. The packages below are checked once a day; installing them is up to you.

                Automatic backups

                Sync between PCs

                Choose a folder that's kept in step between your PCs by OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or similar, and use the same passphrase on each PC. Quasar writes an encrypted file there and merges the other PCs' changes: Spaces, pinned tabs, Favorites, settings, Boosts, Air Traffic Control, history, passwords and addresses. Nothing is sent anywhere by Quasar itself.

                  Tabs on your other PCs

                  The tabs open on your other PCs when they last synced. Click one to open it here. (They're also in the command bar: type part of the title.)
