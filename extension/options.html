Arc+Chrome

Settings save automatically.

Getting started

  1. Pin the extension (puzzle icon › pin), then click it or press Alt+S to open the sidebar.
  2. Put the sidebar on the left like Arc: Chrome Settings › Appearance › Side panel position › Left.
  3. Load a theme from the project's themes folder (Dawn or Dusk) so the window frame matches.
  4. Shortcuts: Ctrl+T Command Bar · Alt+Shift+←/→ switch Space · Alt+Shift+N Little Arc. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Spaces

Each Space gets its own colours, pinned tabs and tab group. Swipe sideways in the sidebar to switch.

Archive

Tabs you haven't looked at are closed and kept in the sidebar's Archive. Pinned tabs and Favorites are never archived.

Air Traffic Control

Send links from a site to a chosen Space automatically. Use a domain (github.com) or a path (docs.google.com/spreadsheets).

Boosts

Restyle any site with your own CSS, applied every time it loads.

Backup

Export your Spaces, pinned tabs, Favorites and settings to a file, or restore them.
